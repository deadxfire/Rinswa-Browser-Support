Help & Support

Find answers to common questions or reach out to our team.

🔧 How do I update Rinswa?

Rinswa checks for updates automatically in the background. If you want to check manually, go to Settings > Help > About Rinswa.

🎨 How do I customize the Cyber-Glass theme?

The Cyber-Glass theme is native to Rinswa. You can tweak the transparency levels and accent colors in Settings > Appearance.

🛡️ Is my data safe?

Yes. Rinswa is built on the Firefox 157 engine but strips out unnecessary telemetry. We believe your local data belongs to you, and ad-blocking is built directly into the browser via native extensions.
