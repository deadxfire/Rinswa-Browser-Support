Universal Theme Compatibility & All Tabs Manager are Live

🎨 How do I switch themes (Light, Dark, and Custom themes)?

Rinswa v1.0.3 introduces full Universal Theme compatibility. Open the App Menu ☰ > Add-ons and themes (or press Ctrl + Shift + A) and select the Themes tab:

  • Rinswa Theme: The signature Cyber-Glass glossy ribbon aesthetic enabled by default.
  • Light Theme: Fully optimized with dynamic high-contrast dark text (#15141a) so tabs, omnibox text, and toolbar icons are crisp and readable on white surfaces.
  • Dark Theme & Alpenglow: High-contrast midnight and dusk dark themes.
  • Custom Themes: Install any third-party theme from addons.mozilla.org seamlessly.

📑 How do I use the All Tabs manager dropdown?

Click the tab list chevron icon (v) at the far right of the tab bar, or press Ctrl + Shift + A. In v1.0.3, the dropdown renders with full tab titles, website favicons, sound playback indicators, and individual close buttons so you can manage dozens of open tabs effortlessly.

🛡️ How does Rinswa's built-in adblocking work?

uBlock Origin is pre-integrated into Rinswa directly at the engine level. No manual extension installation is required. The uBlock shield icon is pinned to your address bar on initial startup, automatically blocking video advertisements, intrusive telemetry trackers, popups, and crypto-miners.

🌄 How do I customize the Alpine Sunset background or choose custom wallpapers?

The signature Alpine Sunset wallpaper is rendered natively with zero flicker. To choose your own wallpaper or adjust blur and transparency, navigate to Settings (about:preferences) > Appearance, or customize your new tab preferences.

🔧 How do I update Rinswa?

Rinswa automatically checks for updates in the background. To check manually, open the App Menu ☰ > Help and Report > About Rinswa. You can also download the latest standalone installers and portable zip archives from our official GitHub Releases.

💻 What are the system requirements?

Rinswa is built for 64-bit Windows 10 and Windows 11. Recommended specifications: 4 GB of RAM (8 GB recommended for heavy multitasking) and 500 MB of available storage space.

🛡️ Is my data safe and private?

Yes. Rinswa is built on the Mozilla Gecko engine (157.0.1) but has all upstream telemetry endpoints, crash pings, and sponsored recommendation services completely severed in code. Your history, bookmarks, and browsing activity never leave your machine.
