Engineered to Outperform

Every component of Rinswa Browser has been tuned for raw responsiveness, uncompromising security, and breathtaking aesthetics.

🎨

Universal Theme & Cyber-Glass Engine

Say goodbye to flat, generic browser chrome. Rinswa features deep frosted glassmorphism, glowing ribbon accents, full support for custom add-on themes, and dynamic high-contrast dark/light adaptability with razor-sharp readability.

Backdrop Filter Universal Themes High Contrast Fix
🛡️

Native uBlock Origin Bundled

No manual addon installation needed. uBlock Origin is baked directly into the standalone installer package manifest, pinned to the navbar, and controllable directly from browser settings.

Ad Blocking Anti-Mining about:preferences
🚫

Zero Sponsored Telemetry

Unlike commercial browsers, Rinswa strips out Pocket, sponsored tiles, telemetry reporting endpoints, and advertising pings. Your browsing habits remain strictly on your machine.

Pocket Removed Zero Telemetry Local Privacy
📑

All Tabs Manager & Smart Groups

Navigate dozens of active tabs with ease. The revamped All Tabs popup displays full tab titles, favicons, audio state, and 1-click closing, paired with workspace tab groups to isolate projects effortlessly.

All Tabs Menu Auto Grouping Full Title View
🌄

Dynamic Alpine Sunset New Tab

A tranquil, uncluttered starting page featuring the high-resolution Alpine Sunset wallpaper, glowing minimalist clock, private search bar, and full support for custom user wallpapers and colors.
